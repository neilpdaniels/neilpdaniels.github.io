Borrow more. Buy less.

Rentalution is a peer-to-peer marketplace that makes it easier to rent items from people nearby. The web platform is live now, with Android availability coming soon.

Visit rentalution.co.uk ↗Contact Rentalution

Designed for the complete rental journey. From finding an item through to closing the booking, each stage stays clear for both people.

How Rentalution works

A straightforward rental journey.

Rentalution keeps the important steps together so borrowers and lenders can see what has been agreed, what happens next and when a booking is complete.

01

Browse and choose

Find an item nearby, review the listing and select dates that work for you.

02

Agree the rental

Confirm the booking details and use the platform to keep both sides aligned.

03

Collect and return

Follow the handover steps and record condition evidence at collection and return.

04

Close the booking

Once the rental is complete, leave feedback and close the transaction cleanly.

Built around confidence

Clear steps for borrowers and lenders.

One shared booking flow

Messages, agreed details and rental actions are connected to the same booking, helping both people follow the journey.

Deposit card holds

Where a deposit is required, Rentalution can place a hold on the borrower’s card so funds are reserved without usually being taken upfront.

Condition evidence

Collection and return steps can include condition evidence, giving both sides a clear record if something later needs to be reviewed.

Feedback at the end

After a rental is wrapped up, feedback helps keep the marketplace useful and fair for the people using it.

Who runs it

Rentalution is operated by Mobberley Labs Ltd.
